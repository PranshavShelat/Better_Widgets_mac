Install

Up and running in a minute.

Better Widgets is free and isn't distributed through the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

Download and unzip

Better-Widgets.zip, then drag Better Widgets into your Applications folder.

Open it once

macOS says it "could not verify" the app. Click Done.

Allow it

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password.

Make it yours

Use the gauge icon in the menu bar: pick a weather location, then Arrange → Grid. Allow Accessibility when asked, so Arrange can see your desktop files.

Or, in Terminal
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Better Widgets.app"
open "/Applications/Better Widgets.app"
FAQ

Questions.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apps outside the App Store need a paid Apple developer certificate to skip that warning. Better Widgets is free and open source, so you confirm it once under Privacy & Security. The full source code is on GitHub.

What permissions does it need?

None are required. Accessibility (optional) lets Arrange see where your desktop icons are so it never covers them. Location (optional) is used only if you choose "Use My Location" for weather.

Does it collect any data?

No. There are no accounts or analytics. The only network request is for weather, which sends just your search text or coordinates to Open-Meteo.

Will it slow down my Mac?

It's built to be light: no Dock icon, efficient system APIs, and an adjustable refresh rate. In Settings → System you can lower the refresh rate or turn off cards you don't need.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu-bar icon and move Better Widgets to the Trash. To also remove its settings, run defaults delete io.github.pranshavshelat.betterwidgets in Terminal.
